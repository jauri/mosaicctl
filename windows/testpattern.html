Mosaic test pattern
F11 or click: fullscreen across the whole wall  ·  H: hide this box
1: moving bars (frame lock / tearing)  ·  2: cell numbers + grid (display order, bezel)
↑/↓: bar speed  ·  ←/→: column count (now )
What to look for: with frame lock working, bars cross every display boundary in one clean line. Steps, a jitter or a horizontal tear at a boundary means displays are not in sync.
